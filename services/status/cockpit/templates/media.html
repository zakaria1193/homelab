Hopping shows

The media autopilot keeps the next two episodes of each show you watch and deletes watched episodes after three days. Shows you watch out of order would be damaged by that, so the ones listed here are never touched: no deleting, no picking the next episodes. Changes apply on the next run, within five minutes.

Maintained movies

A watched movie is announced on Slack, then deleted a week later. Movies listed here are never deleted. Add one before the week is up and the deletion is cancelled; take it off again and the one-week warning starts over.

The lists live in services/media-autopilot/hopping.json and maintained_movies.json; the job is autopilot.py in the same folder.
